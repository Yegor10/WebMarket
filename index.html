The new reality

A three-day immersion into the future of art, music, and human-machine collaboration.

Neon Odyssey is not just a festival — it's a portal to the future. Held annually in NeoPort City's Digital District, we bridge physical reality and the digital void.

Founded in 2072 by the rogue AI collective "Synthetix", the festival celebrates the convergence of humanity and technology. What began as an underground signal broadcast from abandoned server farms has grown into the largest cyber-art gathering on the Eastern Seaboard, drawing over 40,000 attendees from across the Metaverse and physical world alike.

Across 48 continuous hours of programming, you'll encounter electronic symphonies performed by hybrid human-AI ensembles, neural-linked VR installations that respond to your biometric data, and algorithmic art pieces that rewrite themselves in real-time based on crowd movement and ambient sound.

Whether you're a seasoned netrunner, a curious first-timer, or an artist looking to plug into the scene, Neon Odyssey offers a space where boundaries dissolve and new forms of expression emerge from the static.

05

Festival experiences

Beyond the stages — workshops, installations, and spaces to explore between sets.

Dev Bunker Workshops

Learn live coding with TidalCycles, TouchDesigner visuals, and AI-assisted composition. Sessions run hourly from 14:00–22:00 daily. Bring your deck or borrow one on-site. Beginners welcome — no prior coding experience required for intro tracks.

Neural Link Lounge

Try the latest BCI headsets in a guided demo environment. Experience music that adapts to your heartbeat, visuals that follow your gaze, and collaborative VR spaces shared with other attendees. Pro and VIP pass holders get priority booking.

Light District Walk

A self-guided route through twelve permanent and temporary light installations. Pick up a map at any info point. The walk takes roughly 90 minutes and is best experienced after sunset when the district fully activates.

06

Featured artist

Neon Pulse

Originating from the underground data-servers of Sector 7, Neon Pulse blends analog synthesis with neural network algorithms. Their sound draws from 80s film scores, warehouse techno, and the hum of server farms at 3 AM.

Their latest album, "Digital Heartbeat", topped charts across the Metaverse for eleven consecutive weeks. Neon Pulse has headlined Neon Odyssey since 2074 and will premiere unreleased material during the Day 1 Pixel Arena set.
